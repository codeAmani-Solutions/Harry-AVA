Our Mission

HARRY AVA is a media consultancy dedicated to amplifying African voices and perspectives in the American landscape. Founded by broadcast professional Harrison Waweru Kamau, we leverage deep industry experience to bridge cultural gaps, foster understanding, and create impactful content that resonates globally. Our work celebrates the rich diversity and dynamic narratives of Africa.
